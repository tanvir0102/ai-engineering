# One-Day Marathon Training Meal Plan

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete training for a marathon with optimized endurance nutrition:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3100 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (85g), Carbs (410g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (20g), and whole milk (240g). 2 eggs scrambled (100g).
    * Protein: 20g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 85g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (9:00 AM):** White bread (80g) with peanut butter (25g) and jam (15g). Orange juice (250ml).
    * Protein: 10g, Fat: 10g, Carbs: 65g

* **Lunch (12:30 PM):** Grilled lean beef (150g) with brown rice (150g cooked) and roasted sweet potato (180g). Mixed green salad (100g) with olive oil dressing (15ml).
    * Protein: 45g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 95g

* **Mid-Afternoon Snack (3:30 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g) with granola (40g) and berries (80g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 55g

* **Pre-Evening Workout Snack (5:30 PM):** Sports drink (500ml) with carbohydrates and electrolytes, and a bagel (75g) with honey (15g).
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 2g, Carbs: 75g

* **Dinner (8:00 PM):** Baked chicken breast (160g) with whole wheat pasta (150g cooked) and marinara sauce (100g). Steamed broccoli (150g) with olive oil (10ml).
    * Protein: 50g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 70g

* **Evening Snack (10:00 PM):** Banana (120g) with almonds (25g).
    * Protein: 7g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 30g
